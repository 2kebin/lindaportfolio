Who is RHA?

Respiratory Health Association is a 501(c)(3) charitable organization founded in 1906. RHA resides in the public health sector. It’s mission is to “prevent lung disease, promote clean air, and help people live better through education, research, and policy change”. The company started out as the Chicago Tuberculosis Institute in an area where the most tuberculosis outbreaks happened in Chicago, leading the fight against tuberculosis through health checks, education, and political public health reforms. As the threat of TB declined, its mission statement expanded to cover asthma, COPD, lung cancer, tobacco control, Anti-vaping, and air quality.

runs education programs like Fight Asthma Now for students and Courage to Quit, a group program to help adults stop using tobacco. It also funds lung disease research and trains school staff, health providers, and caregivers on asthma management. On the advocacy side, RHA has helped pass major laws, including the Smoke-free Illinois Act, Tobacco 21, school asthma medication protections, and a $25 monthly cap on inhalers.
